1Paper

More arms sense the surroundings better, but get the cell less far.

Trade-off between branching and polarity controls decision-making during cell migration

Liu J, Boix-Campos J, Ron JE, Kux JM, Oremek MEM, Rossi AG, Gov NS, Sáez PJ

Science Advances 12(1), eads2734, 2 January 2026

doi:10.1126/sciadv.ads2734
  • Direction is decided by competition between protrusions that oscillate in a seesaw pattern.
  • Cells oscillate between 1 and 2 junctions (HUVEC), 1 and 3 (macrophages), and 2 and 4 (HUVEC with higher adhesion).
  • Coordinates of tips and junctions were extracted with ResNet50 networks (Methods).

2Figure 1

A vision model went from 258 MB to 8 MB with no loss in accuracy.

  • Bars are drawn to scale. The compressed model is about one thirtieth of the original. Also: 1.91x the throughput, 49% less energy per inference, and SSIM of 0.97 on 20,780 samples.
